Incognito access required

A first-visit measurement needs a profile that holds none of the site's data. In your normal profile the site may already have your consent stored, so no banner appears and the audit would understate what a new visitor receives.

Open this extension's details and turn on Allow in Incognito.

Before consent

—

The page is loaded in a clean window and watched for five seconds without being touched at all — no click, no scroll. Anything that arrives in that window arrived before you could agree to it.

Chrome will show a warning bar

Measuring the earliest requests means attaching Chrome's own debugger, so Chrome pins a “Consent Audit started debugging this browser” bar to the audited window. That is Chrome telling you the truth, not a malfunction. The bar disappears with the window.
